# Private Providers

A graded, filterable worldwide directory of private, patient-pay clinics offering NK-cell, T-cell,
dendritic-cell, stem-cell, or CAR-T immunotherapy **outside any registered clinical trial** — with a
focus on Germany, Mexico, Japan, and Singapore.

- **Dashboard:** open `index.html` (also published as a Claude Artifact).

## Not medical advice

This is a research aid compiled from clinic websites, independent investigative journalism (Science-Based
Medicine, McGill University's Office for Science and Society), academic literature, and patient-community
forums. It may contain errors or be out of date. None of the providers listed are FDA- or EMA-approved for
these uses, none have published randomized outcome data for glioblastoma specifically, and treatment is
paid for out of pocket. **Nothing here is a recommendation of any provider** — several carry explicit
caution flags, and one (Clínica 0-19, Monterrey, Mexico) carries the strongest "serious concerns" tier this
compilation uses, based on a five-part independent investigation finding no credible evidence for its
brain-tumor treatment claims. Discuss any of this with a treating oncology team before acting on it.

## What is included

17 providers across Germany, Mexico, Japan, and Singapore, each showing: what is manufactured, starting
material, estimated cost, insurance notes, an evidence tier (published case series / no independent data /
serious concerns), caution flags where independently reported, contact details, and source links for
outcomes and patient testimonials where they exist.

### Evidence tiers

| Tier | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Published case series | A peer-reviewed or published case series exists for this specific provider |
| No independent published data | Only clinic-published marketing material or case data was located |
| ⚠⚠ Serious concerns | An independent investigation found no credible evidence and/or documented harm |

## How it was built

Compiled via web research (clinic sites, independent critique sites, academic literature, patient forums)
in September 2026. Not exhaustive — absence from this list is not an endorsement, and presence is not
either.
