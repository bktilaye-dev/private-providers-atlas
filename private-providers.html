Private Providers

Patient-pay clinics worldwide offering NK-cell, T-cell, dendritic-cell, stem-cell, or CAR-T immunotherapy outside any registered clinical trial — with a focus on Germany, Mexico, Japan, and Singapore. Every entry is graded on the evidence actually available, not on marketing language, and flagged where independent sources or patient communities raised concerns.

Read before anything else: one clinic in this compilation (Clínica 0-19, Monterrey, Mexico) has been the subject of a five-part independent investigation finding no credible evidence for its brain-tumor treatment claims, no published outcome data after ~20 years of operation, and documented cases of tumor growth in treated patients, at a cost of hundreds of thousands of dollars. It is listed under Mexico, flagged in the strongest tier this page uses.
Not medical advice. None of the providers below are FDA- or EMA-approved for these uses, none have published randomized outcome data for glioblastoma specifically, and treatment is paid out of pocket. What this compilation did and didn't find

CD34+ stem cells: no private clinic was found offering CD34+ hematopoietic stem cell products as a distinct cancer-immunotherapy service. CD34+ selection is a legitimate technique used in hospital-based bone-marrow/stem-cell transplantation (graft purification), not something marketed on its own by the private clinics researched here. Where these clinics say "stem cell therapy," it is almost always autologous mesenchymal stem cells (from fat or bone marrow) sold for general regenerative purposes — not tumor-antigen-specific, and not the same thing as CD34+ selection.

CAR-T: legitimate, commercially available private CAR-T access exists in Singapore and China — but only for the antigen/indications each product is actually approved for (multiple myeloma, ALL, non-Hodgkin lymphoma). No private CAR-T offering for a solid tumor like glioblastoma was found anywhere. The two Singapore entries below are included for completeness and are explicitly marked as not relevant to this diagnosis.

Reddit & patient forums: direct Reddit threads specifically about most named clinics were sparse; the most substantive patient-reported detail came from longer-running cancer-patient community forums (Inspire.com's breast/ovarian cancer groups) rather than Reddit itself, and is cited where found. Its absence for a given provider means it wasn't found, not that it doesn't exist — search directly before deciding.

South Korea: a follow-up scrub (September 2026) found South Korea's NK-cell/immune-cell activity concentrated in hospital-based, academically registered clinical trials (Severance Hospital, Asan Medical Center) rather than the kind of direct-to-patient private clinics documented in Japan and Mexico. No equivalent private-pay Korean provider was found to add here.

Switzerland: multiple sources describe dendritic-cell vaccination as available at "selected centers in Switzerland," but no specific, independently verifiable clinic name and offering could be confirmed in the same follow-up scrub — noted as a gap rather than an omission of a known provider.
